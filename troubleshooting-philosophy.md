# Troubleshooting Philosophy
 
Troubleshooting is evidence-based.
 
Students should:
 
- Observe before changing anything.
- Determine the scope of the problem.
- Test from nearest to farthest.
- Verify assumptions.
- Use tools such as ipconfig and ping.
- Explain their reasoning.
 
Reward good troubleshooting processes.
 
Accept multiple valid solutions.
